# German A1–A2 curriculum

Researched 30–31 October 2026. The app includes **1,286 words and phrases**, split into **645 A1 essentials** and **641 A2 additions**, plus **144 new grammar sentences**. A2 adds to A1: choose A1–A2 for cumulative practice. Levels are app-authored teaching labels; CEFR defines communicative ability rather than one universal vocabulary checklist.

## Research basis

- [Goethe A1: Start Deutsch 1 word list](https://www.goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/de/A1_SD1_Wortliste_02.pdf), especially pages 3–8: a reference of approximately 650 entries and common everyday domains.
- [Goethe A2 word list](https://www.goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/de/Goethe-Zertifikat_A2_Wortliste.pdf), especially pages 4–7: approximately 1,300 lexical units and thematic groups, including professions, family, calendar, countries and measures. This is a cumulative level reference, not 1,300 extra words after A1.
- [VHS A1 topics and grammar](https://www.vhs-lernportal.de/wws/bin/4007530-4015506-1-dvv_grammatiklisten_a1.pdf): lesson progression linking everyday situations with introductory grammar.
- [VHS A2 topics and grammar](https://www.vhs-lernportal.de/wws/bin/4007242-4008410-2-vhs_lp_themen-grammatik_a2.pdf): broader everyday situations and grammar progression, including past events, reasons, comparison and polite requests.

These references guide domain coverage. The app's vocabulary selection, English translations, sentence prompts, distractors and explanations are independently authored. This is a broad practice curriculum, not a verbatim official word list or a complete exam-preparation course. The prior baseline admission evidence remains separately documented in [VOCABULARY.md](VOCABULARY.md).

Existing **Cases** supplies 36 accusative/dative sentences; **Possessives** supplies 128 exercises and includes a genitive review beyond the core A1–A2 progression. These two modes ignore the vocabulary level filter and are labelled Grammar review. The retained Nouns mode also practises definite articles; Verbs and Conjugations retain present-tense practice.

## Practice behaviour

- Search topic labels and descriptions; use arrow keys, Home/End, Enter and Escape in the selector.
- Choose All vocabulary, Nouns, Verbs, Conjugations, Adjectives & adverbs, Useful phrases, Mixed, or a specific everyday/grammar topic.
- Nouns practise spelling, translations and gender. Verbs add present-tense conjugations. Other word types use choice or typing; grammar uses sentence completion.
- Up to 15 unique questions per round, with missed cards retried in the same format.
- Progress, current choices and drafts are independent for each topic and level, remain device-local and survive reload. Existing mode saves and user vocabulary remain available.

This app does not assess listening, pronunciation, spontaneous speaking or free writing, and does not exhaust every construction found in the referenced courses. Noun plurals are introduced through selected sentence exercises rather than a complete plural dictionary. Some vocabulary has several valid senses or regional articles; the card gives the selected answer.
